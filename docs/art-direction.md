# Art direction: night scroll and ofuda

## Seed-derived notes
A random 256-char alphanumeric seed was read for patterns only (it never appears in the game):
- Dense runs of capitals broken by isolated digits suggested a dark, packed field with sparse bright points, which became a night sky with few stars and bright bullets.
- Repeated pairs (`RR`, `XX`, `55`, `vv`) suggested stamped, doubled marks, which became the vermilion seal and tally-stroke meters.
- Long runs without vowels read as terse labels, so the HUD uses short labels and plain numbers instead of icons and containers.
- A few long words against a lot of noise suggested one heavy calligraphic mark against quiet small type.

## 15 one-line directions
1. Hanging scroll: the playfield is a night painting and the HUD is a paper scroll beside it (picked).
2. Ofuda talisman: every UI panel is a paper charm with torn edges.
3. Shrine lantern festival: bullets are paper lanterns drifting upward.
4. Woodblock print: flat cut colors and registration offsets.
5. Ink stone: everything is ground ink, shades from wet to dry.
6. Star chart on lacquer: gold lines on black urushi.
7. Neon arcade cabinet: CRT bezel and scanlines.
8. Weather report from a broken border: isobar maps as bullet patterns (unreasonable).
9. A tax ledger auditing spirits: bullets as ledger stamps (unreasonable).
10. A sewing pattern: bullets are pins, lasers are thread (unreasonable).
11. A cookbook: spell cards are recipes with steamed-bun bullets (unreasonable).
12. A subway map: bosses are interchange stations (unreasonable).
13. Origami: enemies fold and unfold.
14. Stained glass shrine window lit from behind.
15. Bathhouse tile mosaic.

## Pick
1, with the paper material from 2: a hanging scroll at night. The left side is a dark ink-wash sky; the right side is a rice-paper ofuda panel with the calligraphic title 弹幕, plain serif stats, and a vermilion seal that shows the difficulty.

## Build brief
- Aesthetic: Touhou-style shrine night, painted with ink rather than neon.
- Palette: near-black indigo sky; warm rice paper `#e6d9bf`; sumi ink `#1a1210`; vermilion `#d9442b` used only for lives, the seal and danger. Bullets stay high-saturation and high-value so they always stand out against the dark sky.
- Layout: a fixed 4:3 canvas with the playfield on the left and the paper scroll on the right. It scales uniformly with letterboxing, so the full UI is visible at any viewport.
- Type: Ma Shan Zheng for calligraphic headings, Noto Serif SC Bold for body text and numbers, and Noto Sans CJK as the fallback. Fonts are subset from the CSV with `tools/art/subset_fonts.py`.
- Material: torn paper edges, layered ink mountains, tally-stroke power meter, stamp textures. All of it is generated by `tools/art/gen_art.py`.
- Forbidden: purple/blue gradients, glassmorphism, rounded card grids, floating geometric shapes, decorative glows on UI, and Quit buttons on web.
